# Scripting & Storyboarding (PYH-Scoped)

## Ràng buộc PYH (bắt buộc)
- Thay phần `Read these first` bằng tài liệu thật của PYH: `script/script.yaml`, `script/outline.yaml`, `explainer/explainer.yaml`, `profiles/brand.vi.yaml`, và `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-pyh-style-a-mascot-host.md`.
- Chỉ lấy tư duy bảng **audio–visual** và **intent từng cảnh** theo khung **SCENE**; **bỏ** phần quay người thật, gom lịch quay theo trang phục/địa điểm, và **bỏ** WoopSocial / publish ngoài.

## Core Principle

A words-only script plans half the video. The storyboard is a **decision document** answering: *what is on screen at this exact narration beat, and how does it help the viewer understand the sentence?*

## The Framework: SCENE

- **S — Start from the spine**: Follow the approved `script.yaml` and `outline.yaml` chapters—never alter narration wording or medical claims.
- **C — Columns (Audio | Visual)**: Pair every spoken line with concrete visual action (`anatomy`, `process`, `chart`, `illustration`, `kinetic_term`, `mascot_pose`, `CUE_ACTIONS`). Avoid static repeated visuals across consecutive scenes.
- **E — Envision every beat**: Plan visual rhythm (`<= 6s` per beat/change) and `FOCUS_REGIONS` / `LABELS` so the viewer's eye lands on the right mechanism as the line is spoken.
- **N — Number & budget shots**: Keep `chart_crop` within budget (16–24%), ensure sufficient subject diversity (`>= 4` subjects in long-form, `>= 2` in vertical clips), and verify both 16:9 and 9:16 readability.
- **E — Edit on paper first**: Fix pacing, label crowding (≤ 6 Vietnamese labels per SVG), and mascot placement before image generation or rendering.
